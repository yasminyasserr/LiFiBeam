How the frame works

Light off means idle. Each character is sent as one ON start bit, 8 ASCII bits (MSB first, ON = 1), then one OFF stop bit. The receiver waits for the light to turn on, then samples each bit at its midpoint.

Wiring

  • LDR module: VCC to 5V, GND to GND, DO to pin 8
  • LCD 16x2: RS to 12, E to 11, D4 to 5, D5 to 4, D6 to 3, D7 to 2
  • LCD: VSS and RW to GND, VDD and LED+ to 5V, LED− to GND, V0 to a 10k pot middle pin
  • Point the LDR at the phone’s torch or screen, a few centimetres away, and shade it from room light
  • Most LDR modules output LOW in bright light. If your characters come out garbled, set LIGHT_LEVEL to HIGH
